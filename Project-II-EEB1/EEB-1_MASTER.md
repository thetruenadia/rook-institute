# EEB-1 — Master planetary reference
Revision 2026-11-14. Working fictional canon, not a scientific discovery. EEB-1 is a fictional satellite of real Epsilon Eridani b; separate from JT-2. No alternate proper name. ## Materials and terrain
Structural water ice, methane clathrate-bearing formations, complex atmospheric organic sediments, organic sand, solid acetylene and benzene, nitrile-bearing solids, candidate molecular co-crystals and hydrocarbon pore liquids. Co-crystal abundance, mechanical strength and colors not established. Silicates and metals mostly inaccessible below ice crust. Proposed color palette: pale gray/blue clean ice, ochre dust, dark brown dunes, cream evaporites, dark reflective liquids. Artistic rather than spectral.

Terrain area targets: plains 47%, dune fields 20%, mountainous/exposed ice 14%, dissected/labyrinth terrain 11%, evaporite/other depositional 5%, liquids 3%. These are mutually exclusive design targets, not GIS output. Existing numerical maps not updated/verified here.

## History (proposed)
500–490 Ma accretion; 490–450 Ma differentiation; 450–350 Ma crustal deformation and volatile release; 350–150 Ma organic accumulation; 150 Ma–present development of modern hydrocarbon landscapes. Boundaries approximate and overlapping. Origin of life undated.

## Weather and natural phenomena
See [weather design](EEB-1_WEATHER.md). Dense amber haze and hydrocarbon cycle are design foundations. Candidate phenomena: nitrogen effervescence, organic evaporite terraces, dissolution labyrinths, liquid-bearing fractures, shoreline thermal changes and chemical layering. Frequency and magnitude unresolved. No permanent surface liquid water or water glaciers.

## Biology
Indigenous life exists by fictional decision, with methane–ethane solvent as working premise. No validated metabolism, heredity, cellular architecture, species, productivity or origin date. Candidate acetylene + hydrogen reaction is chemically balanced but biochemical implementation unproven. No human discovery timeline.

## Development rules
Use these values for worldbuilding unless revised in the changelog. Do not promote calculations or borrowed Titan analogies into physical proof. Current direction: worldbuilding first; targeted calculations only when needed. Preserve historical failures and separate design from evidence.
