# RI-SIP-001 — EEB-1 Experiment 14
Purpose: test if idealised rain and heat forcing can make a vertical liquid column buoyantly stable or unstable, across two provisional density relationships. Laboratory support: Steckloff et al. (2020), https://doi.org/10.3847/PSJ/ab974e (Titan lake stratification and experimental analogue at 85 K). This is not a replication of TITANPOOL nor physical validation.

Continuity: E13 inputs and source copied in prior_E13; repair.py was separately executed and four files compared to archived E13 (E13_comparison.txt). Earlier withdrawn results not used. E13 density slopes are local finite secants, not validated derivatives. The E13 published benchmark had already been examined during E12.

Method: fixed-mass equal reference layers, Boussinesq-style approximation, 560 kg/m3, constant cp 3000 J/kg/K. State comprises three mass fractions and temperature anomaly. E13 local temperature, hydrocarbon mole-fraction and nitrogen mole-fraction secants transformed to affine mass-fraction derivatives via numerical Jacobian at the provisional reference composition. Diffusive finite-volume exchange with no flux at both boundaries except prescribed forcing. Pool adjacent density-inverted blocks to uniform mass fractions and sensible temperature, instantaneously (not a velocity or turbulence simulation). Rain-equivalent depth 5 or 25 mm at assumed density 450 kg/m3 and T=92 K, x=(0.704,0.176,0.12), replaces equal mass of surface liquid during first day; exported liquid has pre-exchange composition. No literal lake-level rise. Eclipse imposed net cooling 5 W/m2 for 4.2h beginning after day 1, then equal positive energy; not a computed eclipse climate. Extended cooling 1 W/m2 for three days after rain. K same for all tracers and sensible heat (assumed effective mixing, 1e-8 or 1e-6 m2/s). Duration 11.4 Earth days; depths 1,10,100 m; 20 cells, dt=360s; 8 fixed-grid timestep refinement cases and 6 combined spatial/time refinement cases. Parameters in source/results.json. No stochastic inputs.

Checks: three independent species mass ledgers and sensible energy ledger, nonnegative mass fractions, normalisation, stable final density ordering, uniform quiet control, unstable cooled top mixes, stable warm top unchanged. Second execution in separate temporary directory compared six outputs byte-for-byte. No physical validation. Conservation of gravitational/latent/chemical energy is NOT modelled; all species neutral; no chemical reactions. Full thermodynamics, phase equilibrium and gas release not calculated. Rain potentially supersaturated; liquid fixed species is a sensitivity assumption, not a prediction of rain chemistry. No wind, waves, actual convective time, circulation, horizontal advection, bathymetry, evaporation, changing volume, pressure gradients or ice. Coarse grid smears deep surface layers: resolution check shows near doubling of surface contrast in 100m case, so quantitative deep-column outcomes withheld. Timestep checks do not ensure spatial convergence; two linear models are correlated and not a confidence interval. No buoyancy magnitude promoted to canon. No new biology.

Reproduce: python prior_E13/repair.py then python column.py (Python 3.13.5, NumPy 2.3.5, SciPy 1.17.0 for E13). Raw outputs: sweep.csv, timeseries.csv, profiles.csv, timestep_check.csv, spatial_check.csv, results.json. Logs and checksums included. Source dependencies copied from previous experiment. Scientific classification: external evidence (Titan study), executed result (sweeps/controls), conditional model (column), untested hypothesis (storm-created layers on EEB-1), fictional design (reference world), unresolved (natural mixing, phase changes, accurate density derivatives).
