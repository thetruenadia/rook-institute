# EEB-1 — Scientific source index
Starter bibliography from earlier research. Entries are leads, not a claim that every paper or numerical value has been independently checked in this revision. Use DOI/publisher records to verify exact claims before quoting or adopting them. Source list incomplete.

- NASA Cassini / Huygens Titan observations: https://science.nasa.gov/saturn/moons/titan/ — planetary analogue, atmosphere, lakes, dunes and geology.
- McKay & Smith (2005), https://doi.org/10.1016/j.icarus.2005.05.018 — candidate chemical energy for hypothetical Titan life; not proof of life.
- Singh et al. (2017), https://doi.org/10.1016/j.gca.2017.03.007 — cryogenic organic solubility; verify numerical conditions.
- Benzene–ethane co-crystal work, https://doi.org/10.1002/2014GL060531 — laboratory molecular solids; does not establish EEB-1 abundance.
- NIST Chemistry WebBook, https://webbook.nist.gov/chemistry/ — pure-component thermochemistry and vapor pressure; check temperature range.
- Malaska and colleagues, 2016–2017 — nitrogen dissolution and effervescence in Titan-like liquids; exact publication metadata to recover.
- Richardson, Hartwig & Leachman (2018) — density measurements of methane/ethane/nitrogen mixtures; original dataset and measurement uncertainties still need recovery.
- Steckloff and colleagues (2020) — Titan lake stratification; conditions matter, not universal thresholds.
- Hayes and colleagues (2013) — Titan wind-wave research; see E15 README for historical link (not rechecked here).

The real Epsilon Eridani stellar and planetary parameters need a separate current literature check if precision matters. The fictional moon does not have observational data. Historical chat citations not portable to GitHub; replace with durable bibliography as references are verified.
