# EEB-1 — Foundations of indigenous biology
Revision 2026-11-14. Fictional design, not a demonstrated alternative biochemistry. See [master](EEB-1_MASTER.md) and [findings](EEB-1_FINDINGS.md).

## Working canon
Life exists independently of terrestrial life. Its active environment is liquid methane–ethane, often within pores or surface films near 94 K. Earliest and simplest organisms are proposed as surface-associated, porous organic microstructures rather than lipid-bounded cells. Carbon, hydrogen and nitrogen dominate the proposed architecture; actual trace-element requirements remain unknown. No species names or origin date adopted.

## Proposed biological unit
A self-maintaining organic framework contains interconnected solvent-filled channels, selective binding sites, catalytic regions and a heritable molecular pattern. Its own organic framework provides a local boundary; external mineral grains serve as scaffolds or habitats, not as inherently living material. Growth requires environmental feedstock; waste exits into the solvent. This architecture is a fictional design. No demonstrated selective permeability, catalyst, molecular sequence or functional replicator has been identified.

## Metabolism and inheritance
Candidate net reaction: C2H2 + 3 H2 -> 2 CH4. Methane is returned to the environment. Atmospheric photochemistry supplies complex organics and hydrogen; availability, energy coupling, rates and material requirements are unresolved. The reaction is atom-balanced and has published astrobiological motivation, not experimental proof of life. A separate biosynthetic network must build framework material and hereditary components; fuel conversion alone cannot accomplish growth.
Proposed inheritance: sequence-sensitive surface templating transfers a variable organic pattern to new growth; separation or fragmentation produces descendants if they retain functional patterns and rebuild their frameworks. This is a fictional mechanism, not known chemistry. No exact polymer or alphabet selected. Copying accuracy, variation, repair and differential reproduction are required. Crystallisation alone is not life.
Proposed lifecycle: active growth in supplied films; low-activity persistence when supply declines; resumed activity upon replenishment; dispersal by transport of viable fragments. Duration and viability unknown. No claim that cold automatically guarantees longevity.

## Habitat shortlist
Primary candidate: atmosphere-connected capillary fringes and sheltered shoreline sediments with liquid replenishment and access to organic feedstock. Secondary: intermittently wet drainage, floating porous deposits if stable, submerged sediments if reactant access permits. Deep isolated hydrocarbon reservoirs may be solvent-rich but fuel-poor. Distribution is fictional working hypothesis. No confirmed population density, growth rate or biomass.

## Checked external references
- McKay & Smith (2005), Possibilities for methanogenic life in liquid methane on the surface of Titan, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.icarus.2005.05.018 — proposes energetic reactions; kinetics and biological coupling not demonstrated.
- Sandström & Rahm (2020), Can polarity-inverted membranes self-assemble on Titan?, https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aax0272 — computational thermodynamic challenge to azotosome formation.
- Vu & Hodyss (2026), Experimental insights into the azotosome hypothesis in Titan's lake fluids, https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aed1426 — laboratory acrylonitrile–ethane co-crystal and little change with methane; does not establish all possible membrane architectures impossible.

## Unresolved
Molecular catalyst and energy transduction, framework chemistry, heredity and replication, nutrient balance, origin chronology, cell scale, growth/dormancy rates. No new simulations. Next: design the first ecological guilds around distinct environmental resource flows, keeping these gaps explicit.
