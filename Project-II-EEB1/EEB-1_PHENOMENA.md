# EEB-1 — Natural phenomena
Working design, 2026-11-14. Not new simulation outputs.
- Intermittent gas release from nitrogen-bearing liquids during warming, mixing or depressurisation: conditional; actual gas volume and frequency unvalidated.
- Molecular crystal shorelines and former lake-level terraces: adopted fictional design; abundance and strength unknown.
- Dissolution labyrinths in soluble organic sediment: adopted design; not wholesale dissolution of water ice.
- Subsurface hydrocarbon reservoirs, primarily storage in interconnected pores and fractures: adopted design; open caverns and large free-liquid chambers remain hypotheses.
- Floating organic deposits: provisional; require sufficient trapped gas and resistance to wetting.
- Eclipse-responsive environments: conditional on local heat budget and humidity; no automatic fog or freezing.
No new numerical simulations. These features motivate the habitat proposals in [biology](EEB-1_BIOLOGY.md).
